# Example: Cosmetic Product Landing Page Brief

This is a quality reference. Do not copy its product facts into another project.

## Product

A two-unit hair-color mousse designed for simple at-home application.

## Objective

Convert paid-social traffic into purchases of the two-unit offer.

## Audience

Adults seeking a convenient way to refresh visible hair color at home. Mobile-first traffic. Arabic-first market with optional French or English support.

## Primary CTA

Order the two-unit pack.

## Product promise

A familiar mousse application designed to make even distribution easier.

## Evidence available

- Product pack photography
- Application demonstration
- Shade result imagery
- Real customer reviews
- Exact usage instructions
- Delivery and return terms

Any missing item must be marked rather than invented.

## Visual concept

“Pigment in motion”: the page uses a controlled color-diffusion effect that begins as a small liquid detail, travels through macro hair texture, and resolves into a clean product offer.

## Art direction

- Warm ivory base
- Deep espresso typography
- Product-derived pigment accent
- Reflective highlights used sparingly
- Editorial serif display type with a precise sans-serif utility type
- Macro product and hair photography

## Signature interaction

A scroll-linked reveal in which a pigment wave crosses a macro hair surface, transitions from the problem state to the desired cosmetic result, then pulls back to reveal the product pack.

## 3D strategy

Use a real product model only if a convincing GLB exists. Otherwise use high-resolution layered product images with subtle perspective and light movement. On mobile, use a short optimized sequence or static image.

## Page narrative

1. Hero: pack, promise, CTA, verified delivery cue
2. Proof strip: rating or customer count only if supplied
3. Transformation chapter: visual result and honest explanation
4. Mechanism: why mousse distribution is convenient
5. Three-step application
6. Benefits and fit
7. Real customer proof
8. Two-unit offer with product duplication animation
9. FAQ and safety or usage information supplied by the brand
10. Final CTA

## Mobile behavior

- No long pinned scene
- Shorter headline
- Layered image instead of heavy WebGL
- Sticky purchase CTA after the visitor passes the hero
- No proof hidden only in hover states

## Conversion safeguards

- One CTA label
- Offer quantity and price visible
- Shade or variant selection clear
- No fabricated scarcity
- No medical or guaranteed result claims
